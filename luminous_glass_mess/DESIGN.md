## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a crisp, luminous, and tactile digital living space engineered specifically for communal bachelor mess life. It balances financial precision with the warmth of shared domestic routine. The aesthetic fuses sophisticated Glassmorphism and liquid refraction with minimal Scandinavian editorial structure. 

The visual tone is airy, pristine, and optimistic. It dispels the chaotic, stress-laden stereotype of mess management (calculating meal counts, bazaar splits, utility distributions, and cook bills) through immaculate transparency, soothing translucent surfaces, and vibrant financial indicators. 

### Visual Tenets
- **Liquid Luminosity:** Semi-translucent panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) floating over ambient icy-slate backdrops with fine light-refracting edge strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` and `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`).
- **Tactile Clarity:** Tactile pill actions, effortless depth stacking, and clear financial summaries that eliminate roommate friction.
- **Micro-Atmospherics:** Ambient emerald and amber glows bleeding subtly under key cards to indicate cash balance and pending action items.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high daylight legibility, layered glass refraction, and immediate transactional comprehension.

### Roles & Semantic Guidance
- **Primary (`#059669`) & Secondary (`#10B981`):** Represents mess treasury, cleared dues, individual balance surplus, and primary conversion triggers. Emerald carries authority without the cold sterility of traditional banking greens.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber reserved strictly for meal freeze timers, overdue payment reminders, daily shopping rotation alerts, and low-fund flags.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate-900 anchors critical financial figures, member names, and structural headings, preserving contrast across frosted backgrounds.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** Soft icy tint `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Glass Tier 1 (Base Cards):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
- **Glass Tier 2 (Elevated Sheets / Modals):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.18)`.
- **Muted Accents:** Mint wash (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`) and amber wash (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`) used for tag backgrounds and status pills.

## Typography

The pairing blends modern warmth with computational precision:
- **Display & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly, rounded geometry, modern aperture, and clarity at compact sizes on mobile screens.
- **Numbers, Data & Labels (Space Grotesk):** Applied to monetary figures, meal counts, calculation sheets, date timestamps, and badges. Its geometric, slightly technical feel highlights accuracy and accountability in calculations.

### Application Rules
- Currency denominations (BDT / ৳) and transaction summaries must always render in `Space Grotesk` with tabular figures enabled.
- Avoid using pure black; typography relies strictly on Slate-900 (`#0F172A`), Slate-600 (`#475569`), and Slate-400 (`#94A3B8`) for visual tiering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive fluid system optimized for single-hand mobile interactions (the primary form factor for mess members checking meal counts or submitting bazaar costs on the go) and expands to a structured multi-column dashboard on desktop.

### Layout Model
- **Mobile (Base to 640px):** Single column fluid feed with `1rem` outer margins. Cards occupy full width with compact vertical gaps (`space-md`). Sticky navigation docked at the bottom with frosted liquid glass.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Quick stats and today's meal roster render side-by-side.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12-column fixed-max layout (max-width `1280px`) with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer canvas padding. Divided into persistent mess overview (left), primary data ledger (center 7 columns), and activity feed/shopping roster (right 3 columns).

### Rhythm
Components follow strict 4px/8px incremental spacing. Internal card padding is consistently `space-md` for compact cards and `space-lg` for primary summary widgets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism, liquid refraction, and ambient diffused shadows instead of hard structural dividers or stark drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Stacking
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Smooth gradient mesh spanning `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`, interrupted by faint out-of-focus emerald and icy blue ambient light spots (`filter: blur(80px)`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Glass Cards):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`, backdrop filter `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, and shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Pinned Items):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, backdrop filter `blur(20px)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`, and dynamic shadow: `0 12px 36px -6px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Sheets):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`, backdrop filter `blur(24px)`, shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

### Border Treatment
Borders simulate light refraction. Every glass panel uses a semi-translucent light gradient stroke (top: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, bottom: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`), evoking smooth beveled glass.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tailored Level 2 (Rounded) aesthetic, offering tactile softness that complements liquid glass aesthetics without feeling overly childish or toy-like.

- **Cards & Surface Containers:** `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) for mobile cards; `rounded-xl` (24px / 1.5rem) for large mess analytics panels.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) for quick toggles, actions, and user avatars; `rounded-md` (8px / 0.5rem) for input fields and tabular ledger items.
- **Nested Elements:** Inner items maintain proportional radius hierarchy (e.g., an inner element inside a 16px radius card uses an 8px radius to preserve optical harmony).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Deposit / Confirm Meal):** Emerald linear gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #10B981 0%, #059669 100%)`), text white, pill-shaped, subtle drop shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.3)`. Hover state lifts by `1px` with increased saturation.
- **Secondary (Add Expense / Cancel):** Frosted glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, text Slate-900, pill-shaped.
- **Destructive / Alert (Meal Off / Freeze):** Frosted amber tint (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`), border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`, text `#B45309`.

### Cards & Ledger Containers
- **Meal Counter Card:** Floating frosted surface with a large tabular count, stepper controls (`+` / `-`) encased in micro-glass bubbles, and an amber countdown badge showing cutoff time.
- **Treasury Balance Card:** Tinted translucent emerald mesh backing, high-contrast Slate-900 currency display, and a miniature avatar stack of paid versus pending members.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.6)`, inner shadow `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- On focus: Background becomes pure white, border transitions to `#10B981`, and a diffused ring appears (`0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact height (24px), font `label-sm`, rounded full.
  - *Active / Paid:* `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)` fill with `#047857` text.
  - *Pending / Bazaar Duty:* `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` fill with `#B45309` text.
  - *Guest Meal:* `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.1)` fill with `#334155` text.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded-md (checkbox) or circle (radio), border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state animates with an emerald spring pop, displaying a crisp white icon over `#059669`.

### Specialized Mess Components
- **Daily Bazaar Ledger List:** Compact stacked rows separated by hair-thin translucent divides (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`), featuring buyer avatar, item category icon, timestamp, and Space Grotesk currency figure.
- **Quick Switcher (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner):** Frosted glass segmented control capsule with an animated sliding white pill indicator.
